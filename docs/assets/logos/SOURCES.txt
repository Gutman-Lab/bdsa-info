Institution logos used on info.bdsa.io.

These files identify the institutions participating in the Brain Digital Slide
Archive. Institution names and logos are trademarks of their respective
institutions; they are used here only to identify participating sites, not to
imply endorsement.

All files are SVG and were retrieved on 2026-11-16 from Wikimedia Commons or
the English Wikipedia. Where a file is hosted on Wikipedia as non-free, the
project uses it solely for identification of the institution in a factual list
of consortium members.
